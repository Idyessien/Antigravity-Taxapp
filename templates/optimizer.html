How this works in Nigeria:
When you earn interest on a Federal Government (FGN) Bond or Treasury Bill, you pay 0% tax—you keep every kobo. However, when you earn interest on a Bank Fixed Deposit, Commercial Paper, or Savings App, the bank or tax authority deducts Withholding Tax (WHT) or Income Tax from your profit. Use this tool to see which deal actually puts more money in your pocket.
